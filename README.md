## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **CS student at FAST NUCES Peshawar** with hands-on experience in **Unity game development**, **AI/ML engineering**, and **freelance video editing**. I've shipped real products — from browser-based 3D configurators to real-time emotion detection systems — and I thrive at the intersection of technical depth and creative execution.

- 🎮 Junior Unity Developer @ **Cognitive Force** — ported a full 3D mobile app to WebGL/browser
- 🧠 Built **EmoSense**, a real-time AI emotion detector using YOLOv8 + PyTorch trained on RAF-DB
- 🎬 Freelance **Video Editor** on Upwork specialising in AI-topic content
- 🎓 Currently pursuing **BS Computer Science** (2022 – Present)
- 📍 Based in **Peshawar, Pakistan**
